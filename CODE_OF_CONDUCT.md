# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Focus feedback on ideas and contributions.
- Protect private information and report security concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberately disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
